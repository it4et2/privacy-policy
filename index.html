Privacy Policy

Notification SMS Forwarder
Effective date: October 3, 2026

Summary: This app reads incoming SMS notifications and forwards their text to an email address you choose. Everything stays between your phone and Gmail's SMTP servers. We do not run a server, we do not collect analytics, and we do not sell or share your data with anyone.

1. Who we are

Notification SMS Forwarder ("the App", "we", "us") is an Android application developed and published by an independent developer. The App's purpose is to automatically forward incoming SMS notification content to an email address that you, the user, configure inside the App.

This policy explains what data the App accesses, how it is used, where it goes, and what control you have over it.

2. What data the App accesses

2.1 SMS notification content

The App uses Android's NotificationListenerService to read the content of notifications posted by messaging applications installed on your device (for example, Google Messages, Samsung Messages, or AOSP Messages). This includes the sender's phone number or name and the text of the message.

The App does not read your SMS database directly. It only sees the content that your messaging app chooses to display in a notification. If your device is configured to hide sensitive notification content on the lock screen, the App will only see the redacted placeholder instead of the full message.

2.2 Contact names

If you grant the READ_CONTACTS permission, the App looks up the sender's phone number in your device's contacts so that the forwarded email can show a friendly name (for example, "John Doe") instead of a raw phone number. Contact data is queried locally and never leaves your device except as the resolved name included in the forwarded email.

2.3 Email credentials you provide

To send emails, the App requires:

  • Your Gmail address (as the sending account)
  • A 16-character Google App Password
  • The target email address where forwards should be delivered

3. Where your data is stored

All settings — the target email, sender Gmail address, and App Password — are stored locally on your device in Android's private SharedPreferences storage. This storage is sandboxed by the Android operating system and is not accessible to other apps.

We do not transmit, upload, or back up your credentials to any server we control. We do not have a server.

4. Where your data is sent

When an SMS notification is captured, the App opens a direct SMTP connection from your phone to Google's mail servers at smtp.gmail.com:587 using STARTTLS encryption. The message content is transmitted to that server and delivered to the target email address you configured.

This is the only network transmission the App performs. There are no other outbound connections.

5. What we do NOT do

  • We do not run any backend servers or databases.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not collect crash reports or usage statistics.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not access your SMS database, call logs, location, camera, microphone, or files.
  • We do not read notifications from apps other than the messaging apps listed in the App's source code.

6. Third-party services

The App uses Google Gmail SMTP to deliver emails. When your message content passes through Google's servers, it is subject to Google's Privacy Policy. We do not control Google's handling of that data.

No other third-party services are used.

7. Data retention

The App does not retain message content. Messages are transmitted once and immediately discarded from the App's memory. Only your configuration settings persist on the device until you uninstall the App or clear its data.

8. Your rights and controls

You can revoke any of the App's capabilities at any time:

  • Notification access: Settings → Apps → Special access → Notification access → toggle the App off.
  • Contacts access: Settings → Apps → Notification SMS Forwarder → Permissions → Contacts → Deny.
  • All local data: Uninstall the App, or clear its data from Settings → Apps → Notification SMS Forwarder → Storage → Clear data.

Because the App does not operate a server, there is no remote data to delete.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. The App is a personal productivity tool for adults.

10. Security

App credentials are stored in Android's private SharedPreferences sandbox. SMTP connections use STARTTLS encryption. We recommend using a Gmail App Password rather than your primary account password, and revoking it at any time from myaccount.google.com/apppasswords.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated. Material changes will be noted in the App's Play Store listing description.
